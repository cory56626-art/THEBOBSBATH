# LATCH — The Appeals Desk

[Play the appeals game](https://cory56626-art.github.io/THEBOBSBATH/appeals/)

Investigate 25 fictional ban appeals over five increasingly difficult shifts. Retrieve original evidence, pin findings, and decide whether to restore an account, uphold a ban, or escalate the case. Includes career and practice modes, autosave, investigation notes, explained audits, and desktop/touch controls. [Source, controls, and tests](appeals/README.md).

# Meridian — Open City

[Play the game](https://cory56626-art.github.io/THEBOBSBATH/)

An original 3D coastal city game with driving, pedestrians, police response, hostile NPCs, and a Civic Square scenario. Free to play, no ads, no account. Desktop keyboard/mouse and phone touch controls are supported.

This branch contains the tested production website served by GitHub Pages. The previous site files have been replaced.

[Editable source, tests, and controls](https://github.com/cory56626-art/THEBOBSBATH/tree/game/meridian-city) live on the new `game/meridian-city` branch. Source verification passed 19 physics/simulation tests and three production browser flows, covering desktop controls, missions/NPC behavior, and phone touch controls.

Click Enter the city, use WASD to drive or walk, E to enter/exit your vehicle, Space to brake, right mouse to aim, F or left click to fire, R to reload, and Escape to pause. Use Performance graphics for slower devices.
