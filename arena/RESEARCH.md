# Film research and simulation notes

This independent fan project uses the **MCU film versions of Hulkbuster, Hulk and Ultron**. Venom uses **Tom Hardy’s Sony film version**: his MCU appearance is a crossover cameo, not an MCU combat demonstration. Film appearances are the primary reference; linked character summaries and production material provide supporting detail.

## Hulkbuster and Veronica

*Avengers: Age of Ultron* establishes Mark XLIV’s massive red-and-gold modular armor, hydraulic punching mechanism, repulsors, flight assistance, restraints and replaceable components. The Johannesburg fight shows Hulk damaging the armor and Veronica delivering replacement hardware. Veronica is a support deployment system, not an unlimited healing power.

- [Mark XLIV equipment and film appearances](https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Man_Armor%3A_Mark_XLIV)
- [Licensed Hot Toys Hulkbuster construction and visual reference](https://www.sideshow.com/storage/product-images/903803/product-instructions-hot-toys-hulkbuster-deluxe-version-sixth-scale-figure_pdf_5e97af31c1e37.pdf)

The game gives Hulkbuster punches, a jackhammer burst, repulsor fire, assisted movement and replaceable damaged limbs. Veronica’s **six-module inventory, vulnerability and delivery timing are gameplay rules**, not quantities or specifications established by the film.

## Hulk

The savage film Hulk demonstrates immense strength, jumping, durability, healing and a powerful thunderclap. He can be injured and incapacitated; he is not invulnerable. Rage influences his fighting behavior, but the films do not establish a measurable, unlimited strength multiplier.

- [Hulk’s MCU appearances and abilities](https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Hulk)

The simulation includes punches, leaps, a thunderclap and healing. **Regrowing a severed arm is not demonstrated in these MCU films.** It is an optional speculative extension, enabled by default to honor the requested mechanic. Its duration is game tuning.

## Venom

The Sony films show powerful close combat, bites, tendrils, rapid shape changes, host protection and healing. Fire and strong sonic exposure are weaknesses. The visual reference is black organic skin, pale eyes, veins, teeth and a long tongue, without importing the comics’ white spider emblem.

- [Director’s fight-scene breakdown, Motion Picture Association](https://www.motionpictures.org/2018/10/watch-venoms-director-breaks-down-a-fight-scene/)
- [Sony’s Venom film page](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/venom)

## Ultron

*Avengers: Age of Ultron* establishes robotic strength, flight, energy blasts, gravity-related manipulation, networked bodies and increasingly durable construction. His bodies and sentries can be dismantled. No Infinity Stones, comic-only adamantium or unrestricted material generation are assumed.

- [Ultron’s MCU appearances and abilities](https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Ultron)
- [fxguide production interview: Ultron’s rigid plates and visual effects](https://www.fxguide.com/fxfeatured/casting-the-vendors-on-avengers/)

## Physical and visual limits

Combat uses rigid bodies, impulses, collisions and detachable parts. Walking uses motor assistance and animation; this is not a fully emergent biomechanical simulation. Masses, attack forces, damage thresholds, repair counts and healing durations are gameplay tuning rather than film measurements. Blood and metallic debris distinguish biological injury from machine damage. Procedurally constructed models provide **stylized film-inspired likenesses**, not photoreal production assets. Outcomes are simulated, without a predetermined winning character.


## Expanded film roster and balance rules

- **Carnage** uses *Venom: Let There Be Carnage*: red-and-black symbiote material, tendrils, formed blade weapons, rapid reforming, and sonic/fire vulnerability. [Sony film page](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/venomlettherebecarnage) and [official trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ezfi6FQ8Ds).
- **The Thing** uses *The Fantastic Four: First Steps*: Ben Grimm has orange rock-like skin, extraordinary strength and resistance to bombardment. His game abilities are physical charges, grabs and slams, with no energy beams or symbiote regeneration. [Disney D23 film character guide](https://d23.com/meet-the-characters-of-marvel-studios-the-fantastic-four-first-steps/).
- **Wolverine** uses healthy Hugh Jackman movie traits and the yellow/blue *Deadpool & Wolverine* costume: adamantium claws and skeleton, aggressive close combat and accelerated healing. His reinforced limbs resist ordinary separation; he can still be temporarily incapacitated in this simulation. [20th Century Studios film description](https://www.20thcenturystudios.com/movies/x-men-origins-wolverine) and [film portrayal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadpool_%26_Wolverine).

The requested **Go to Sleep gas** is implemented as a custom, probabilistic short-range gameplay ability. Its chemical composition, odds and 2–3.2 second limp duration are game rules, not a confirmed measurement of film equipment. Ultron is immune. Successful organic targets resist immediate repeated exposure; Wolverine recovers sooner.

**Flight limits are requested balance rules:** Hulkbuster receives 2.8 seconds and Ultron 3.5 seconds of powered flight, followed by landing and a 14-second recharge. Their film technology does not establish these numerical fuel limits.

**Health and balance:** the displayed match health is the defeat threshold. Local head and torso damage no longer produces a hidden instant defeat at high displayed health. A single damage event cannot remove more than 6.5% of maximum match health. Armor durability, Hulk rage multipliers, regeneration rates, military firepower and all matchup tuning remain game settings; outcomes are never assigned by character name.


## Titan Breaker — original character design

**Source:** the user’s complete supplied specification and reference image, titled **Titan Breaker**, created by **Elias Vane**. This is an original character design, separate from the Marvel film research above. The supplied appearance is an 11 ft / 3.35 m muscular humanoid machine with metallic dark-grey overlapping plates, enormous shoulders and forearms, reinforced legs, mechanical fists, cyan eyes and a central cyan reactor. Its purpose is to capture and restrain Hulk-level threats alive.

### Armor and autonomous decisions

Zeno armor is slightly stronger and more durable than Marvel’s vibranium. It resists impacts, crushing, explosions and conventional weapons, and reinforces vital mechanical areas. It is **not indestructible**: sufficient force can dent, deform, fracture or remove plates and components. Titan Breaker is not guaranteed to overpower every Hulk-level opponent.

The onboard artificial intelligence controls movement, target tracking, tactical positioning, defense, weapon switching and restraint decisions. It avoids unnecessary civilian casualties and prioritizes nonlethal containment. The machine is silent. Sunday does not contain, supply or command its primary AI.

### Established offensive systems

- **Mechanical strength:** basic punching strength slightly exceeds the original MCU Hulkbuster. Heavy punches, kicks, gripping, two-handed grabs, lifting, tackles, throws, body slams and sustained restraints are part of its physical repertoire.
- **Piston punches:** both arms can deliver very fast consecutive mechanical strikes. If both arms use pistons, neither palm may fire repulsors simultaneously. One arm may use pistons while the opposite hand fires a repulsor.
- **Red Thruster punches:** red thrusters behind the elbows accelerate individual high-impact strikes. These are distinct from pistons and cannot be combined with the piston mechanism in the same attack.
- **Palm repulsors:** either hand can discharge concentrated energy at short or long range, pushing threats away or adding pressure. Their allowed one-arm pairing with opposite-hand pistons does not permit dual-piston repulsor fire.
- **Homing missiles:** each forearm holds exactly **15**, for **30 total**. Armor flaps normally conceal the compartments and open for guided launches. The machine may fire small quantities rather than discharging the whole stock, and can limit firepower to support capture alive.

### Restraint, defense and movement

The **Zeno restraint rope** deploys rapidly from the back of a hand, wraps large portions of the target’s body, tightens, restricts arms and legs, and maintains pressure while adjusting around a struggling target. It distributes restraint rather than relying on one exposed limb. It is durable but can break under sufficient force. Sustained successful restraint is a live-capture outcome rather than a killing attack.

Small **wrist shields** supplement the armor by protecting and blocking around hands and wrists during close combat and grappling. **Air-suction foot stabilizers** resist ground knockback and unwanted displacement during heavy impacts; they are primarily useful while grounded.

The supplied propulsion design supports **sustained controlled flight**, including vertical takeoff, altitude changes, pursuit, rapid approaches and landing. Titan Breaker is therefore not assigned Hulkbuster’s or Ultron’s user-requested short-burst flight limitation.

**Optical stealth** manipulates reflected light to reduce visual detection for concealment, approaches and repositioning. It does not guarantee complete invisibility or defeat all sensor types.

### Sunday and the water weakness

**Sunday** is an independent aerial support platform operating substantially higher than Veronica. It remotely scans condition, monitors damaged or missing sections, and dispatches replacement components on powerful Red Thrusters. It is a repair support platform, **not Titan Breaker’s brain**. Any game inventory, delivery timing, energy figures or durability numbers are explicitly simulation tuning; they are not new canonical capacities added to the supplied design.

**Large amounts of water are the primary established weakness.** Heavy flooding, deep-water environments and substantial exposure can compromise effectiveness. The specification does not name the affected internal systems, so the game should represent compromised performance without claiming a newly invented internal failure mechanism. The battlefield-water setting provides dry ground or optional **2.4 m flooding** for testing this weakness. Zeno protection does not imply water immunity.

No additional weapons, transformations, powers, guaranteed wins or universal stealth immunity belong to this character. Quantitative game tuning must preserve the specified arm conflicts, finite missile stock, breakable armor/restraints, independent AI, live-capture objective and water vulnerability.
