    FIELD GUIDE

    A thinking hunter.
    A chance to escape.

    You’re an observer. Both characters act on their own. The survivor searches for three escape objects, then unlocks the front door. Being caught costs a life; found objects stay collected.

    What the resident knows

    It sees within a forward cone. Walls and furniture block its view. Footsteps, picking up objects, and dropped objects can draw it to a sound. It searches nearby closets and beds, and its memory of a lost chase fades.

    How the survivor escapes

    It chooses routes, runs from danger, breaks sight at corners, and hides in closets or under beds. Hiding works best when the monster didn’t see it happen. Three lives give it a difficult but possible way out.
