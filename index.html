City behavior

Civilians follow sidewalks and flee danger. Police investigate gunfire and pursue you for crimes. Hostiles attack nearby targets. Buildings block line of sight.

Good to know

M Expand map · ESC Pause
Avoid civilians and traffic. Break police line of sight to lose your wanted level. Reset a stuck vehicle from the pause menu.
