THE METHODWhat are we actually measuring?+
01

Keep the brain constant.

All three modes use the same pretrained model. Intuition selects directly; search explores continuations; selective mode decides whether to explore. A result is evidence about this setup, not a rating against every chess engine.

02

Count the actual work.

Record elapsed decision time, model evaluations, search nodes, and chosen moves. A time cap is a maximum, not a promise of equal work. Browser speed and background tabs affect timings.

03

Separate learning and testing.

A controller should be evaluated on held-out positions. Paired openings reverse colors. A small match or a puzzle score cannot establish a reliable Elo rating.
