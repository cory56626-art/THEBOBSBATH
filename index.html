    ORIGINAL HERO / ELIAS VANE

    TITAN BREAKER

    An autonomous, silent 3.35 m heavy assault and containment machine. Metallic dark-grey Zeno plates protect cyan eyes and a central cyan reactor. Zeno is slightly more durable than vibranium, but enough force can dent, fracture or tear it away. Titan Breaker seeks live capture and avoids unnecessary civilian harm.

    Its tools are heavy strikes, grappling, wrist shields, palm repulsors, sustained flight, ground-based air-suction stabilizers, optical stealth and a breakable Zeno rope that wraps the target’s body. Stealth reduces visual detection; it does not defeat every sensor. Large amounts of water are its primary weakness.

    TITAN BREAKER / ARM LIMITS

    POWER WITH LIMITS

    Each forearm holds exactly 15 homing missiles: 30 total. Launchers sit beneath opening armor flaps. Firepower can be limited for nonlethal capture.

    Dual-arm piston combinations prevent palm-repulsor fire. One arm can use pistons while the opposite hand fires a repulsor. Red Thruster punches accelerate individual strikes and cannot combine with piston punches in the same attack.

    Sunday scans for damage from higher altitude than Veronica and dispatches replacement components on Red Thrusters. Sunday supports repairs; Titan Breaker’s onboard AI makes its own decisions. Neither armor nor restraints guarantee victory.

    01 / MARK XLIV

    HULKBUSTER

    Heavy modular armor, repulsors, hydraulic jackhammer fists, powered leaps and arm restraint. Veronica supplies replacement armor. Flight lasts 2.8 seconds. He must land and complete a 14-second recharge before flying again. “Go to Sleep” releases close-range gas with a chance to cause a short limp ragdoll state. Robots are immune. Veronica carries six repair modules.

    02 / GAMMA

    HULK

    Savage MCU Hulk: immense strength, rage, leaps, healing and the thunderclap. Injuries reduce limb function. Slow severed-limb regrowth is an optional extension, enabled by default.

    VENOM

    Tom Hardy’s movie Venom: tendril grapples, powerful bites, leaps and host healing. Fire and strong sonic shocks hurt the symbiote. This design has pale eyes and veins, without a spider emblem.
