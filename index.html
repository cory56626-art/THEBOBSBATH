      How PRIMORDIA works

      1. Design a body out of nodes and muscles, and give it a name. Nodes have grip, size and mass; links are either springy muscles or rigid bones.
      2. Evolve spawns a whole population of that body. Each one gets a small recurrent neural network — a nervous system — wired at random, so generation 1 is pure flailing.
      3. Every creature lives out the trial. The best are kept, bred together, and mutated. Repeat. Walking, hopping and swimming are not programmed anywhere — they are discovered.
      4. Use Arenas at once to choose how many run side by side, Speed to fast-forward, and Turbo to grind generations with rendering off.
      5. Turn on Body drift to let the shape mutate too — limbs grow, shrink and fall off across generations.
      6. Archive is a time machine: race generation 1 against generation 200 in the same arena and watch what changed.

      Reading the arena

      Muscles glow hot as they contract and cold as they stretch. Node colour is grip: orange grips, blue slides. A gold flag marks the record to beat, and posts along the ground are one metre apart.

      Everything runs locally in your browser. Your creatures are saved to this device only.
