How it works

Every ball holds a constant speed and never stops. When a weapon connects it deals damage and grows — longer reach, more damage, faster fire. Fights start tame and end in chaos.

Damage fills a super meter. When it tops out, the ball unleashes its ability's super automatically. Last ball alive wins.
