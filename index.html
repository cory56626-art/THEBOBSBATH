FIELD NOTES / READ BEFORE DARK

Survive another night.

01 — Gather

Look at supplies and press E or USE. Food, water, medkits, and salvaged materials restock at dawn. Eat with 1, drink with 2, heal with 3.

02 — Make a shelter

B opens building. Choose a barricade (3 material) or a light (2). R rotates. Click / USE places. Barricades slow entities and can be broken. X dismantles one you’re looking at.

03 — Trust your radio

Invite Mira, Dex, and Sol in the menu. T opens chat. Ask questions or give orders: follow, wait, gather, build, heal, or find a secret. They navigate, share supplies, and help you recover.

04 — When it goes dark

Sprint with Shift. Space jumps. Click / PUSH shoves a nearby entity. Light slows the Watcher. Still Lifes copy your suit and words, but their proportions and movements are wrong.

05 — Explore

M opens your field map. Find the chair assembly, pool annex, records office, conservatory, and replica home. There is no final wave: outlast your best run.

06 — Stay connected

Create a lobby, copy its invite, and let friends join before or during the run. The host must keep the tab open. Internet lobbies use PeerJS signaling and WebRTC; restrictive networks may prevent joining.
