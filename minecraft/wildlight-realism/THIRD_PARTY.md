# Provenance

All material generation code, material artwork, pack lighting settings and the geometric pack icon were authored for this project. No third-party shader pack or texture artwork was copied.

`source/biomes.json`, `source/flipbooks.json` and `source/block-texture-paths.json` contain compatibility metadata derived from the **Mojang/bedrock-samples** stable branch for internal version **1.26.50.4**, September 15, 2026. These records preserve vanilla biome components, animation registrations and texture resource names. Mojang's files are subject to the [Minecraft End User License Agreement](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula); see the [upstream notice](https://github.com/Mojang/bedrock-samples/blob/main/LICENSE.md). They are not included under a separate license granted by this project.

PBR and lighting formats follow Microsoft's public Minecraft Creator documentation. Numpy, SciPy and Pillow are build dependencies only; they are not bundled into the `.mcpack`.
